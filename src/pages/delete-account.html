Ask about your account and data

Email AustinMeridianHQ@gmail.com with the subject “FitQuest account and data deletion request.” Briefly describe what you want removed. We may need to verify ownership before handling an account-related request; the verification procedure and completion time have not yet been established.

Contacting us starts a conversation about your request. It does not trigger automatic deletion, and this draft cannot promise complete or immediate removal. Do not send passwords, login tokens, recovery links, or backup codes.

Signing out is different from deletion

Signing out ends remembered access and offline account access on the device. It does not delete your Supabase account, cloud save, or local game progress. The app attempts to revoke the device’s notification registration when signing out; alerts already delivered to the operating system cannot be recalled.

Removing the app or clearing its storage can remove app-local data depending on the operating system. That does not by itself remove server-side accounts, cloud saves, shared records, or copies in provider backups. Exported backup codes and reports can remain wherever you saved or shared them.

What the deletion process must address

  • Account identity and authentication/session data.
  • Private cloud saves and any applicable local save controls.
  • Social profile, friendships, guild and raid memberships, activity records, and shared aggregate contributions.
  • Push registrations and notification queues.
  • Support correspondence, service-provider records, backups, and any justified retention exceptions.

Exact deletion coverage, treatment of shared history, verification, retention exceptions, and backup expiry are unresolved. This website project does not implement mobile or backend deletion changes.

Before this becomes a public deletion resource

The operator needs to establish and test a request workflow, verify server-side removal and any retained records, set realistic handling periods, and add appropriate in-app controls. This status page should not be represented as a completed app-store deletion mechanism.
