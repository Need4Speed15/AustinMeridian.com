COMMON QUESTIONS

Common questions.

Is FitQuest available to download?

FitQuest is currently in development and testing. It is not publicly available on the App Store or Google Play, and there is no announced launch date. This website does not distribute test APKs.

How do I keep my daily streak?

Complete all three Daily Challenge workouts and claim the daily bonus before the local day ends. Completing exercises opens adventures, but claiming the bonus is also required to keep the streak. Missing a full day breaks the current streak; previously collected rewards remain yours.

Where is my progress saved?

Completed actions save locally on your device. Settings pages with Save Changes keep edits as drafts until saved. Signed-in accounts sync a private cloud save when connected.

Check Account → Account & Cloud Save and use Sync Now before switching devices. Offline changes must upload first. If two saves conflict, you choose one; progress is not merged. Backup codes contain an unencrypted save, so keep them private.

Can I play offline?

An existing local adventure can continue offline after a successful sign-in on that device, including when a remembered token expires. A new device requires a connection to retrieve the save. Online friend/guild actions and shared-raid updates require a valid online session. Reconnect to sync progress.

How do workout targets work?

Open Account → Enabled Workouts to choose exercises, manage custom exercises, and set Comfortable and Max rep targets. Changes apply only after Save Changes. Record the exercises you complete; the current gameplay does not import Apple Health or Health Connect activity.

Why aren’t notifications arriving?

Check Account → Notifications, phone notification permission, Quiet Hours, and your device’s Focus or battery settings. Social alerts require a connection and a push-capable standalone/development build; remote push is unavailable in Expo Go. Delivery is not guaranteed by an in-app preference alone.

What are FitQuest+ and the ads in test builds?

FitQuest+ is a membership prototype with no real billing connected. The Android test integration requests Google demo interstitials at eligible breaks; live monetization is disabled. iPhone, web, and Expo Go do not request ads through that integration. Installed test builds can differ from the latest source.

How do I report a problem?

In the app, open Report a Problem to create a reviewable report, then copy or share it if you choose. Nothing uploads automatically. You can email support with the app version, device model, what happened, and steps to reproduce it. Review notes and attachments for private information first.

How do I delete my account or data?

A complete account-deletion flow has not yet been verified or established. Signing out does not delete your account or saved progress. Read the current status and contact options.
