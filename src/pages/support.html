COMMON QUESTIONS

Common questions.

Is FitQuest available to download?

FitQuest is currently in development and testing. It is not publicly available on the App Store or Google Play, and there is no announced launch date. This website does not distribute test APKs.

How do I keep my daily streak?

Complete all three Daily Challenge workouts and claim the daily bonus before the local day ends. Completing exercises opens adventures, but claiming the bonus is also required to keep the streak. Missing a full day breaks the current streak; previously collected rewards remain yours.

Where is my progress saved?

Completed actions save locally on your device. Settings pages with Save Changes keep edits as drafts until saved. Signed-in accounts sync a private cloud save when connected.

Check Account → Account & Cloud Save and use Sync Now before switching devices. Offline changes must upload first. If two saves conflict, you choose one; progress is not merged.

Backup and recovery limits

Backup codes contain an unencrypted save, so keep them private. Current provider configuration does not include scheduled project backups or point-in-time recovery; cloud sync is not a guaranteed recovery service.

Can I play offline?

An existing local adventure can continue offline after a successful sign-in on that device, including when a remembered token expires. A new device requires a connection to retrieve the save. Online friend/guild actions and shared-raid updates require a valid online session. Reconnect to sync progress.

How do workout targets work?

Open Account → Enabled Workouts to choose exercises, manage custom exercises, and set Comfortable and Max rep targets. Changes apply only after Save Changes. Record the exercises you complete; the current gameplay does not import Apple Health or Health Connect activity.

Why aren’t notifications arriving?

Check Account → Notifications, phone notification permission, Quiet Hours, and your device’s Focus or battery settings. Reconnect to receive online social alerts. Delivery is not guaranteed by an in-app preference alone.

Build requirements for social alerts

Social alerts require a connection and a push-capable standalone/development build; remote push is unavailable in Expo Go.

What are FitQuest+ and the ads in test builds?

FitQuest+ and advertising are test features. No real billing or live monetization is enabled. Eligible adult Android test builds use Google demo ads; active FitQuest+ suppresses those ads.

Platform, age, and testing details

FitQuest+ is a membership prototype with no real billing connected. The Android test integration requests Google demo interstitials at eligible breaks; live monetization is disabled. iPhone, web, and Expo Go do not request ads through that integration. Updated source blocks ads for unknown-age and under-18 users; adult ads remain demos and active FitQuest+ suppresses them. Android demo-ad load, display, close, and same-screen/audio resumption passed owner phone checks; live ads remain disabled. Installed test builds can differ from the latest source.

How do I report a problem?

In the app, open Report a Problem to create a reviewable report, then copy or share it if you choose. Nothing uploads automatically. You can email support with the app version, device model, what happened, and steps to reproduce it. Review notes and attachments for private information first.

How do I delete my account or data?

In a build with the updated controls, open Account → Account & Cloud Save → Your Account → Delete Account. Verify with your current password and the DELETE confirmation. You can also request removal manually by email without reinstalling. Signing out does not delete your account. Read the steps, verification process, and retained-data limits.

Build availability and testing limits

The updated source adds Delete Account under Account → Account & Cloud Save → Your Account, with current-password verification and DELETE confirmation. The operational report covers Android build-20 form/shared-deletion checks, Android build-21 cleanup/restart, and current iPhone source in Expo Go. Older builds may lack these controls. Physical interruption after verified submission remains unverified.
