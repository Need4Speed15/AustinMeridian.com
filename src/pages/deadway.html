SURVIVAL MEETS THE MIDWAY

Stay for the games.
Survive the night.

Warm bulbs, worn paint, and something moving in the shadows. Step into an American carnival after closing, where familiar attractions become the backdrop for first-person zombie survival.

Fight off the next wave, then put your aim to work at a carnival game. In the development prototype, timed attractions can keep running while you step away to fight.

Solo play and cooperative networking are implemented in the prototype. Separate-computer and internet multiplayer readiness remain unverified.

IN THE PROTOTYPE

The foundations are playable.

First-person movement, shotgun combat, zombie pursuit, a three-wave survival prototype, solo play, cooperative host/join networking, and three carnival games.

Networking has been tested in separate local processes. This does not establish internet-ready multiplayer, matchmaking, or Steam integration.

STILL AHEAD

More of the park to bring to life.

Further environment detailing, combat and economy balancing, animation polish, optimization, and accessibility work remain in development.

Additional attractions, broader equipment progression, and a finite escape with optional continued survival are directions for the game, not current availability.
