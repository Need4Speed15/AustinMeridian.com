The public study tool requires no product account. Bookmarks, recall history, review queues, and saved sessions are stored in your browser.

A STUDY AID, WITH CONTEXT

Keep current references close.

Educational study aid only. Verify against current FAA regulations and publications, your aircraft’s current operating documents, and a qualified flight instructor. Self-ratings do not establish proficiency or training credit.

Sources and limitations

Explanations link to FAA publications, eCFR regulations, and other aviation sources for further checking. A citation is not a guarantee that every answer is current or independently validated. No guaranteed review cadence is established.

Aircraft-specific material may cite archived documents, including a 2009 CTLS AOI. Check applicability to the aircraft, its current supplements, placards, and installed configuration. The material primarily concerns U.S. aviation concepts.

No FAA approval, official endorsement, certification, flight-review/IPC/WINGS training credit, or equivalence to required instruction is claimed.
