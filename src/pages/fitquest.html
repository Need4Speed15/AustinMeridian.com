ONE REP. ONE STEP FORWARD.

How FitQuest works.

In FitQuest, you record the exercises you actually complete. Those workouts power your adventurer’s journey: from daily challenges to fantasy encounters, unfolding quests, and shared raids.

01

Set your pace

Choose enabled exercises and adjust your workout targets to suit your routine.

02

Put in the effort

Complete your daily exercises and record your reps to open the day’s adventures.

03

Move the story forward

Use exercise sets to attack, earn rewards, and progress through your quests.

WHERE WE ARE NOW

Development status.

FitQuest is in active development and testing. The features described here reflect the current source build and may change before release. Installed test builds can differ.

FitQuest+ is a membership prototype; real billing is not connected. The Android advertising integration uses Google demo ads, with live monetization disabled. There is no announced public launch date.
