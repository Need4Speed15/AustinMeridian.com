A LITTLE ORDER IN THE EVERYDAY

The details of your month,
in one considered place.

For personal and household bill keeping, with manual control over the records you enter.

01 / DUE DATES

A view that suits your day.

Switch between a bill list, calendar, and upcoming-due view. Organize one-time and recurring bills with categories and labels.

02 / PAYMENT HISTORY

A record you can revisit.

Record full or partial payments, correct an entry, and undo a change. “Paid” means recorded by you; Folio doesn’t execute or verify payments.

03 / MANUAL BALANCES

Your own running picture.

Track assets, debts, dated balances, and recorded net worth. Values are manually entered estimates, without bank connections or live investment data.

YOUR RECORDS, ENTERED BY YOU

Keep the context
with the numbers.

Folio stores entered bills, amounts, dates, notes, payment records, and tracked balances locally. No login service or bank connection is part of the current app.

Recording a payment doesn’t automatically adjust your tracked account balances. You maintain those balances separately.

Backups and exports

Save a JSON backup for restoration or export CSV records for spreadsheet use. Backups are saved manually through your device’s file and sharing menus; there is no automatic cloud backup or cross-device synchronization.

Backup JSON contains readable financial information and is not password-encrypted by Folio. Choose and protect its destination carefully. A local recovery copy alone doesn’t protect against losing the phone.

Restore and local deletion

Restoring a backup replaces the matching profile rather than merging it. Local profile resets and removal of app-managed backup copies are available. Files you saved elsewhere remain under your control.

Comfort and reminders

The current build includes reduced motion, high contrast, text scaling, and optional local reminders. Signed-build notification interactions and broader spoken-accessibility testing still need verification.
